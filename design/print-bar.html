Print progress and Save as PNG

Study for review items 21 and 22. A print now runs as a job the editor can follow: drawn, sent, then watched label by label as the printer prints. This page shows how the print bar could show that, where Cancel goes, and where a Save as PNG button could sit.

Decided, 2026-11-12, and built: A, words with a thin line along the bar, and Print turning into Cancel while a cancel still works; S1, a Save PNG button beside Print; and "Printed" in the text colour with a ✓, not the green that's too light for text. Built as shown, except that the bar names the next label, "Drawing label 121 of 400", while it's being drawn.

What the editor can know

StageWhat's countedCan it be cancelled?
Drawinglabels drawn and converted, by the app itself: exactyes; nothing has been sent
Sendingnothing to count; brief, except for very long jobsno
Printinglabels as they come out, from the printer's printing phases: seen with 3 labels, cut on and cut offno: the printer has the job
Printedevery label counted, and the printer idle—
Sentthe job reached the printer, but the count couldn't be followed: no status, or fewer phases than labels—

A · Words, a thin line, and Print turns into Cancel recommended

The status line says where the job is, in the words of the table above. A 2 px line along the top of the print bar fills as labels are drawn, then again as they print. While Cancel can still stop the job, the Print button becomes Cancel; once it can't, it waits, disabled, until the job is over.

For: one place to look, and Cancel is where your pointer already is. Against: the Print button changes its job, so a double-click on Print could cancel; the editor would ignore a second click within half a second.

B · Words only, with a separate Cancel

No line. A small Cancel link sits beside the words while it can act, and Print stays Print, disabled.

For: Print never means anything else. Against: a small target for the one thing you'd want quickly, and a long job's progress is a number you have to read.

Save as PNG

S1 · A button beside Print recommended

Shown in the bars above: a plain button, the same height as Print, never filled. It saves the label in the preview, as printed: the row shown in the preview, without the editor's hatching for empty codes, at 300 dpi with that size stored in the file, so it prints at its real size from another program. The file is named after the template and the row, such as Bin label, row 3.png, or label.png with no template name.

S2 · In the Templates panel

Beside Save, as "Export…". Out of the way, but it's a picture of one label, not of the template, so it'd sit oddly there.

To decide

  2. Save as PNG: S1, a button beside Print, or S2, in the Templates panel.
  3. "Printed" and "Sent" in the text colour with a ✓, in place of the green that's too light: yes or no.
